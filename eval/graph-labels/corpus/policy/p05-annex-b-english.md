# Annex B — English Summary

This annex is a non-binding English summary of the SLA terms. In case of any discrepancy, the Chinese original prevails.

## Section 1 Scope

This annex applies to enterprise customers who signed a purchase agreement. Member-facing availability commitments are set out in Section 2, and the compensation schedule is described in Section 3.

## Section 2 Availability

Monthly availability is calculated as (total minutes − unavailable minutes) ÷ total minutes. Unavailability is counted from the moment the customer opens a ticket until service is restored.

See Section 4 for the claims process and the list of exclusions.

## Section 3 Compensation

If monthly availability falls below 99.9% but stays at or above 99.0%, the compensation is 10% of the monthly service fee. Below 99.0%, the compensation is 30%.

Compensation is issued as service-fee credits and is never paid in cash. The cap described in Section 2 applies to every claim.

## Section 4 Claims and Exclusions

Customers must submit a claim within 15 business days of the incident, together with the ticket number, the affected request volume and a loss statement. Claims filed after that window are waived.

The exclusions listed in Section 1 of the SLA terms apply to this annex as well.
